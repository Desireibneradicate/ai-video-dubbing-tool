## 🌍 Your Voice, Any Language

**AI Video Dubbing Tool** is a cutting-edge, fully automated dubbing platform designed to make your video content globally accessible. 

Gone are the days of hiring expensive voice actors or struggling with robotic text-to-speech. This tool combines state-of-the-art Speech-to-Text (Whisper), neural machine translation, and **Zero-Shot Voice Cloning** to dub your videos seamlessly. It captures the exact tone, emotion, and cadence of the original speaker, but speaks it in a completely different language.

Whether you are a YouTuber trying to reach a global audience, an e-learning creator, or a filmmaker distributing your work internationally, this tool handles the entire pipeline. Upload a video, select your target language, and watch as it generates perfectly synced lip movements and cloned audio.

> 💡 **Why use this dubbing tool?** Because localization shouldn't cost a fortune. Expand your audience globally without losing the unique personality of your voice.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. No installation required.**

### 1️⃣ Launch the web app
Click the **Try Dubbing** button at the top of this README to open the web interface.

### 2️⃣ Upload your video
Drag and drop an MP4 or MOV file (up to 1GB). Select your target language from the dropdown.

### 3️⃣ Download your dub
Hit "Generate". The AI will extract the audio, translate it, clone the voice, and stitch it all back together.

## ⚙️ How It Works

<div align="center">

| Layer | Responsibility | Tech |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 🎤 **Transcriber** | Extracts text and timestamps | `OpenAI Whisper` |
| 🧠 **Translator** | Context-aware translation | `DeepL / GPT-4` |
| 🗣️ **Voice Synth** | Clones voice and speaks new text | `ElevenLabs / RVC` |
| 🎬 **Video Renderer** | Merges audio and syncs lips | `Wav2Lip / FFmpeg` |

</div>

The entire pipeline runs on a scalable cloud infrastructure. We use FFmpeg to strip the audio, neural networks to clone the voice, and proprietary algorithms to align the new audio with the original video timeline.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Does it really sound like the original person?</b></summary>

Yes! With just 10 seconds of reference audio, the Zero-Shot voice cloning model can replicate the timbre, accent, and speaking style of the original speaker almost perfectly.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How long does processing take?</b></summary>

As a rule of thumb, a 10-minute video takes about 2-3 minutes to process, depending on server load and the length of the speech.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are my videos private?</b></summary>

Absolutely. Your uploaded files are processed securely and are **automatically deleted** from our servers within 24 hours. We do not use your data for training.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I dub copyrighted content?</b></summary>

You should only use this tool on videos you own or have permission to translate. Dubbing copyrighted content and re-uploading it may result in copyright strikes on YouTube.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include your browser version, the target language, and a screenshot of the error log.
</details>
